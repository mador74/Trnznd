Supported providers

Providers you can connect

Connect the accounts you already hold, or open new ones with the providers below to get money in and out, and to buy and sell assets. Every account stays yours, held with the provider you chose.

How it works

  1. Open an account with a provider

    Sign up directly with the provider. Its own terms, identity checks and fees apply.

  2. Connect it to Trnzit

    Add the API details from your provider account. You can revoke them at any time.

  3. Manage it under your policies

    See balances and history in one view, and send instructions under your approval rules.
