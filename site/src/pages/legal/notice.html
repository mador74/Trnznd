About Trnznd and ZEND

Trnznd, S.A. is a Panama-incorporated financial technology company developing digital settlement infrastructure for cross-border commerce. ZEND is a fully backed, multi-currency digital settlement asset designed to facilitate payments, settlement, treasury operations, and value transfer across participating users and businesses. Access to Trnznd services is subject to applicable onboarding, identity verification, compliance, and eligibility requirements.

Important Notice

ZEND is designed as a settlement and treasury utility asset and is not intended to be marketed, offered, or used as an investment product, security, collective investment scheme, deposit, savings product, or speculative instrument. ZEND does not provide ownership rights in Trnznd, entitlement to profits, dividends, interest, voting rights, or any expectation of financial return. Users should acquire, hold, and use ZEND solely for its intended settlement, treasury, liquidity management, and payment functionality.

While ZEND is designed to reduce exposure to the volatility of any single currency through a diversified reserve methodology, no asset is entirely free from risk. Users should carefully review all available documentation, including the Risk Disclosures, Terms & Conditions, Compliance Overview, and relevant legal notices before using the platform.
