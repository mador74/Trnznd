Reserve transparency

Reserves you can verify

Every Zend in circulation is backed by a minimum of 100% reserves, in cash and cash equivalents.

  • Segregated custody: reserves held structurally apart from Trnznd’s operational accounts
  • Professional management of the reserve
  • Independent assurance: attestations and audit reports, published here
