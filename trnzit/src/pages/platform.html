02

Non-custodial by design

We never hold your private keys. We never hold your assets.

Trnzit and the Trnznd Group never hold private keys and never take custody. Your assets stay with the exchanges, custodians and wallets you chose. You keep control and custody at all times.

  • One login, one point of contact across all of your providers
  • Revocable access: Trnzit stores the API details you provide to connect each account. You can revoke them at any time, from Trnzit or at the provider itself
  • No pooled funds: nothing passes through a Trnzit account, because there isn’t one

For organisations, Trnzit’s approval policies are designed so that no single member can act alone. Individual account holders keep sole control of their own accounts.

Authorised members

The right people, with the right authority

Business users can add members with different authority levels, so Trnzit mirrors the governance your organisation already has, rather than replacing it.

  • Separate who can view, who can initiate and who can approve
  • Set approval limits per member and per type of action
  • Remove access instantly when someone changes role or leaves

Teams: 5 users with standard roles. Premium: 10 users with custom roles. Enterprise: as many as you need. [Confirm role names]

05

Invoicing & direct settlement

Invoice, settle and reconcile in one flow

Issue invoices to counterparties and arrange direct settlement with other Trnzit users. Payment details come from a whitelisted address, not from an email, which means fewer errors and less room for fraud.

  • Invoices carry a verified, whitelisted destination
  • Settlement follows each side’s own approval policies
  • Paid invoices reconcile automatically against history

Settlement is always executed by your own custodian or wallet, on your instruction. Trnzit never transmits or holds funds.

06

Multi-signature wallets

Multi-sig, set to your own compliance policy

Use multi-signature wallets configured to your organisation’s own compliance and control policies, with M-of-N approvals and address whitelisting enforced before anything is signed.

  • M-of-N approvals: for example, 2 of 3 for routine payments and 3 of 5 above a threshold
  • Address whitelisting: funds can only go to addresses your approvers have agreed
  • Policy changes need approval too, so the rules can’t be quietly rewritten

07

Trade & convert

Trade in your own exchange accounts, from one interface

Trade and convert assets in the exchange accounts you already hold, without logging in to each one. Orders go to your exchange and are executed there, under your approval policies.

  • Quotes and execution come from your exchange, not from Trnzit
  • Large trades can require approval, just like payments
  • Every trade appears in your consolidated history

08

How it connects

Secure APIs carry your instructions. Your providers do the rest.

When you approve an action in Trnzit, the instruction is passed through a secure API to the connection you selected: your exchange, custodian or wallet. The provider executes it. Custody never moves.

{{icon:connect}}

You choose the connection

Every instruction names the provider that will carry it out. Nothing is routed elsewhere.
