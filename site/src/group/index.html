The products

What the group offers

{{logo}}

Zend, by Trnznd S.A.

A digital asset with true global stability

Zend holds fully backed reserves that significantly reduce volatility against most global currencies. It is particularly useful for:

  • Cross-border merchants with delayed settlement terms
  • Treasury departments protecting against currency fluctuations
  • NGOs with long-window distributions
  • Individuals who want to hold value outside a single domestic currency, acquiring Zend through other platforms
  • Fully backed reserves
  • Low volatility against most global currencies
  • Direct minting and burning for organisations only. Individuals acquire or dispose of Zend through other platforms.
Visit Zend {{i:arrow}}

Trnzit, by Trnznd Technologies

A platform with every access point in one view

Trnzit consolidates the digital wallets, exchanges and other providers its users rely on into a single viewpoint and a single log-in, for individuals and organisations alike.

  • Total balances across every connection, on one page
  • Control the movement of stablecoins and other supported assets across multiple chains
  • Governance controls that suit each organisation’s guidelines
  • Non-custodial: never holds keys, custody or control of users’ assets
