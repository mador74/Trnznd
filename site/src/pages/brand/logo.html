ZEND logo set

The Trnznd Enso in solid Teal #00D4AA, with the lowercase zend wordmark and the descriptor “Global Stability, by Trnznd”. It follows the same pattern as the Trnzit lock-up, so the group’s marks sit together.

How it was built. The Enso and the letters “z”, “n” and “d” are traced from the Trnznd master logo (the n–d overlap is kept for the trnznd logo only). The “e” is new: a round bowl with the same outer size and stroke weights as the “d”, a crossbar the weight of the “z” bars, and an open lower terminal. A designer should check the “e” and its spacing against the master artwork before final release.

Usage

  • Clear space: on all sides, at least the wordmark’s x-height (the height of the “n”).
  • Minimum size: 80px wide on screen. Below that, use the Enso symbol on its own.
  • Colour: the ZEND Enso is always solid Teal #00D4AA. The wordmark is Midnight on light backgrounds and white on dark. The Trnznd parent logo keeps its gradient Enso.
  • Don’t stretch, rotate, recolour, outline, add shadows or effects, place on busy backgrounds, or set the wordmark in a font.

Across the group: Trnznd uses the gradient Enso, ZEND solid Teal, and Trnzit solid Electric Blue.
