## 3. Brand Positioning

TRNZND is institutional-grade financial infrastructure for cross-border settlement. The ZEND token is backed by a deeply researched, multi-currency blended reserve of fiat currencies — eliminating single-currency dependency, reducing volatility by up to 70%, and cutting transaction costs from dollars to pennies.

Positioning in the market:

- **Category:** currency-neutral stablecoin / B2B settlement infrastructure.
- **Against USD-pegged stablecoins:** ZEND removes the need to hold and hedge a currency you neither need nor want. Value is stabilised globally, not anchored to one economy's monetary policy.
- **Against traditional correspondent banking:** instant settlement, low cost, no FX chain friction.
- **Differentiator with purpose:** reserve yield is channelled directly into social impact projects — "Purpose Beyond Payment" is structural, not marketing.

Tone: authoritative, not arrogant. Innovative, not hype-driven. Purposeful, not preachy. Precise, not cold. Never crypto-hype language ("to the moon", "game-changer", "zero risk").

## 4. Target Audience

**Primary:** B2B — international institutions and businesses conducting cross-border trade.

- Treasury and finance teams at import/export businesses managing multi-currency exposure.
- Financial institutions and payment providers seeking currency-neutral settlement rails.
- Cross-border traders and trading houses who lose margin to FX volatility and conversion chains.
- Institutional portfolios seeking a low-volatility digital settlement asset with regulated infrastructure.

**Audience mindset:** risk-aware, compliance-driven, efficiency-focused. They evaluate on stability data, reserve transparency, and regulatory posture — not hype.

## 7. Typography Direction

Brand guide spec: primary typeface **Helvetica**, secondary **Inter** (data/mono contexts).

Web implementation (Google Fonts):

- **Headings & body: Inter** — the brand's own secondary typeface and the closest premium Google-Fonts match to Helvetica. Use Bold (700) for Display/H1–H3, Regular (400) for body.
- **Data & numerics: IBM Plex Mono or JetBrains Mono** — for reserve figures, token amounts, wallet addresses, and block data (e.g. `ZEND 1,000.00 | 0x7f3a...8b2c`), echoing the brand guide's transaction-data styling. Render in Teal on Midnight.

Type scale (from brand guide): Display 42px Bold · H1 32px Bold · H2 24px Bold · H3 18px Bold · Body Large 14px · Body 12px · Caption 10px · Overline 8px UPPERCASE. For the landing page, scale proportionally upward for hero display sizes while keeping the hierarchy ratios.

## 8. Logo Direction

The primary logo combines the **Enso symbol** with the lowercase **trnznd** wordmark.

- **The Enso:** a brush-drawn open circle in the brand gradient (Teal → Blue → Violet), drawn in a single deliberate motion. It represents continuous movement, balance, and intentional openness — value flowing freely and securely across borders, systems, and markets. Its open form signals a system that is never static, never closed, always improving.
- **Wordmark:** custom lowercase "trnznd" — black on light, white on dark.
- **Variations:** icon mark (favicons, small scale), wordmark only (inline/legal), stacked (square formats).
- **Rules:** approved brand colors only; maintain clear space; never stretch, recolor, rotate, add effects, or place on busy backgrounds. Minimum 80px width digital — below that, icon mark only.

## 9. Product Description

**ZEND** is a currency-neutral stablecoin engineered for cross-border trade.

- **Purpose:** minimise cross-border volatility and stabilise global value transfer for institutions and businesses trading internationally.
- **Reserve design:** backed by a deeply researched, multi-currency blended reserve of fiat currencies. No single-currency dependency — stability is diversified by construction, cutting volatility by up to 70% versus single-currency exposure.

- **Regulated infrastructure with purpose:** institutional-grade transparency, with reserve yield channelled into social impact projects.

## 14. Adobe Firefly Video Brief

**Project:** TRNZND landing page hero — cinematic background loop
**Duration:** 15–30 seconds, seamless loop
**Aspect:** 16:9 (3840×2160 master; 1920×1080 delivery), safe to crop for mobile
**Mood reference:** institutional, calm, precise — "global trading floor after hours"

### Concept

A slow, cinematic journey across a digital wireframe Earth as glowing trade routes illuminate between continents — value flowing freely, stably, without friction. The film should feel like premium financial infrastructure: quiet power, engineered precision, zero hype.

### Scene description

- **Setting:** deep Midnight-blue void (`#0A0F1E`) with a faint architectural grid and subtle orbital dot patterns receding into darkness.
- **Hero element:** a digital wireframe Earth rendered in fine, luminous lines — Teal (`#00D4AA`) primary strokes with Electric Blue (`#0088FF`) secondary detail and rare Violet (`#6C3AED`) highlights on network nodes.
- **Trade routes:** slender arcs of light rising off the globe's surface, connecting financial centres across continents. Each arc draws itself in a single deliberate motion (echoing the Enso), pulses softly at its destination node, then settles into a steady glow.
- **Particles:** subtle wave particles — a fine drift of luminous points flowing along the routes like currents of value; restrained density, never busy.
- **Lighting:** soft, glowing cinematic lighting; low-key with gentle volumetric bloom around the globe's rim. High contrast, desaturated environment with subtle teal color grading.
- **Camera:** slow macro camera movement — begin close on a single glowing route node (shallow depth of field), ease outward and orbit gently to reveal the full wireframe Earth and its network of routes. Movement is continuous, smooth, and unhurried; no cuts if possible, or one imperceptible dissolve.
- **Atmosphere:** premium and institutional. Vast negative space, calm rhythm, engineered precision. The mood of sovereign-grade financial infrastructure — stability made visible.

### Motion & pacing

- Overall tempo: slow and deliberate; nothing moves quickly.
- Route arcs ignite in a staggered, breathing rhythm (one every 2–3 seconds).
- Particle drift is continuous and subtle, giving the loop life without distraction.
- Loop point: camera orbit and route cycle return to the opening framing.

### Color script

- Background: Midnight `#0A0F1E` → near-black vignette edges.
- Primary luminance: Teal `#00D4AA` (routes, globe strokes).
- Secondary: Electric Blue `#0088FF` (detail lines, data pulses).
- Accent (sparing): Violet `#6C3AED` (node highlights) — never more than two accents dominant in frame.

### Usage notes

- The video sits behind hero typography — keep the upper-left and centre-left regions low-detail so white display type and CTAs remain legible.
- No text, logos, or UI elements baked into the video.
- Must grade cleanly under a dark overlay (10–30% Midnight scrim).
