About Trnznd

Global trade requires a truly global asset

Trnznd builds currency-neutral settlement infrastructure for cross-border commerce. We issue ZEND, a fully backed, multi-currency digital settlement asset, so businesses can settle in value rather than in someone else’s currency.

What we believe

Tomorrow’s financial infrastructure shouldn’t look like yesterday’s

Nor should it be bound by the geopolitical constraints of today.

Cross-border trade still runs on a handful of national currencies. Businesses outside those economies hold and hedge money they neither need nor want, and lose margin to conversion chains and slow correspondent banking.

By untethering settlement from the volatility of any single fiat currency, we help enterprises operate with confidence across borders, and focus on growth rather than FX mitigation.

Purpose Beyond Payment

Reserve yield, channelled into social impact

Our purpose is structural, not a slogan. Yield earned on the ZEND reserve is directed to social impact projects around the world.

ZEND holders do not receive this yield, and ZEND carries no entitlement to profits, interest or financial return. Read the important notice.
