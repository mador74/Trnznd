The problem

More providers, more logins, more risk

Holding assets across many providers is sensible. Running them through many separate logins, each with its own controls, is not.

{{icon:login}}

Scattered logins

Every provider has its own portal, its own users and its own approval rules.

{{icon:user}}

Key-person risk

Too often, one person can move funds on one platform without anyone else signing off.

{{icon:history}}

Manual reconciliation

Month-end means exporting from every provider and matching it by hand.

{{icon:mail}}

Payment-detail risk

Destination details arrive by email and are typed in by hand, which invites errors and fraud.

Governance

One policy, applied to every provider

Define who can initiate, who can approve and how many approvals each action needs, once. Trnzit applies the same rules whichever exchange, custodian or wallet carries out the instruction.

  • M-of-N approvals by amount, connection or action type
  • Whitelisted destinations for payments and settlements
  • Initiators can’t approve their own requests
  • A full record of every request, approval and policy change
