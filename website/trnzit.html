Why Trnzit

Digital-asset operations shouldn't mean giving up control.

Most corporate teams face a false choice: hand assets to a custodian, or share a private key across a spreadsheet of people. Trnzit removes the trade-off — self-custody with the controls a finance team actually needs.

Custodial risk

Third-party custody means counterparty exposure and someone else's controls over your money. Trnzit is non-custodial — assets never leave wallets you own.

Shared keys

A single key passed between staff is a single point of failure. Multi-sig policies mean no one person can move funds alone — approvals are enforced on-chain.

No audit trail

Finance needs a defensible record. Every transaction, approval, and policy change in Trnzit is captured in a complete, exportable history.

01 — The Platform

Non-custodial multi-sig, built for treasury.

Spin up wallets your company controls on EVM chains and Solana. Set the signing policy — 2-of-3, 3-of-5, whatever your controls require — and Trnzit enforces it at the protocol level. You manage the treasury; users never get direct, unilateral access to the wallet.

02 — Controls

Funds only move where you allow.

Whitelist the external addresses your treasury is permitted to transact with — suppliers, exchanges, sub-accounts. Payments in USDT, USDC, ZEND, or other supported assets can only leave to approved destinations, on the chains you choose. Everything else is blocked before it starts.

03 — Roles & Authorities

The right people. The right permissions.

Add your team and assign authority by function — who can initiate a payment, who can approve one, who can manage the whitelist, who can only view. Separation of duties is enforced by the platform, so control of the treasury never rests with a single user.

04 — Audit

Every movement, on the record.

Track the full transaction history of every wallet you own — amounts, counterparties, chains, and the exact signers who approved each transfer. A complete, exportable ledger for reconciliation, reporting, and audit, backed by the immutability of the chain itself.
