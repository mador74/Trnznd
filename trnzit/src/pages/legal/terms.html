Trnzit is a technology platform only. Trnzit and the Trnznd Group do not provide custodial, money transmission, payment, exchange or investment services, and never hold client assets or private keys. All transactions are executed by your own chosen providers on your instructions.

Normal prices: Basic $290/year or $29/month; Premium $990/year or $99/month; Institution $2,490/year or $249/month. New users, first 12 months: Basic $149/year or $14.99/month; Premium $499/year or $49.99/month; Institution $1,249/year or $124.99/month. A new user is an organisation or individual that has never had a Trnzit account. 4. Free plan and free trials

The Free plan has no end date. Basic, Premium and Institution plans paid by card, Google Pay, Apple Pay or PayPal include a 30-day free trial. Your payment details are authorised when you sign up, but no payment is taken. Cancel within 30 days and you pay nothing; otherwise your first payment is taken on day 31. Plans paid in USDT or USDC, monthly or annual, are paid in advance; if cancelled within 30 days, the payment is refunded in full.
