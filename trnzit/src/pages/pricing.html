Trial & billing terms

Clear terms, before you start

{{icon:card}}

Paying by card

You get a 14-day free trial, which you can cancel at any time within the 14 days. Before the trial starts, a card pre-authorisation hold of $15 is placed on your card. [Confirm when the hold is released]

{{icon:clock}}

After the trial

To cancel, give at least 30 days’ notice. Your service ends on your renewal date. [Confirm how notice is given, and what happens if notice falls inside 30 days of renewal]

{{icon:coins}}

Paying in USDT or USDC

There is no standard free trial. Instead, you get a 14-day trial limited to one exchange or wallet connection. [Confirm which networks/chains are accepted for payment]

{{icon:convert}}

Changing plan

Upgrade at any time: your new plan and contract start immediately. If you upgrade part way through an annual plan, a new 12-month plan starts at the higher price, and the unused part of your current plan is credited against it. [Confirm how upgrades on monthly plans are charged] Downgrades take effect at your next renewal date.

Set-up takes minutes. As soon as your payment is confirmed, you can start connecting providers and inviting your team.
