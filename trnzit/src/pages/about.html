Why Trnzit exists

Centralised control shouldn’t mean centralised custody

Organisations now hold value across banks, exchanges, custodians and wallets. Managing it usually means either juggling a dozen logins or handing assets to a single intermediary.

Trnzit takes a third route. It brings every account into one governed view and one point of contact, while keys and assets stay exactly where you put them. Your providers keep doing what they do well; Trnzit makes them work as one system, under your rules.
