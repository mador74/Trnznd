08 / How it connects

Instructions travel. Custody doesn’t.

Trnzit sits between you and the providers you already use. It holds your instructions, policies and approvals. Your exchange, custodian or wallet holds your assets and executes, exactly as it does today.

Governance

No single person moves your assets alone

For organisations, every material action can require more than one person. Set who can initiate, who can approve, and how many approvals each type of action needs.

  • M-of-N approvals by amount, connection or action type
  • Address whitelisting so funds can only go where you’ve agreed in advance
  • Separation of duties: initiators can’t approve their own requests
  • A full record of who requested, who approved and when
