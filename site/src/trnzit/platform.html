02

Non-custodial by design

We never hold your private keys. We never hold your assets.

Trnzit and the Trnznd Group never hold private keys and never take custody. Your assets stay with the exchanges, custodians and wallets you chose. You keep control and custody at all times.

  • One login, one point of contact across all of your providers
  • Revocable access: Trnzit stores the API details you provide to connect each account. You can revoke them at any time, from Trnzit or at the provider itself
  • No pooled funds: nothing passes through a Trnzit account, because there isn’t one

For organisations, Trnzit’s approval policies are designed so that no single member can act alone. Individual account holders keep sole control of their own accounts.

05

Address whitelisting

Funds only go where you’ve agreed in advance

Save the addresses you pay, and Trnzit will only send instructions to those addresses. Payment details come from your whitelist, not from an email, which means fewer errors and less room for fraud.

  • Whitelist addresses on EVM-compatible chains, Solana and Tron
  • On Business and above, changes to the whitelist can require approval
  • Every addition and removal is recorded
  • Create payment requests on every plan. From Business up, attach a PDF such as an invoice

Payments are always executed by your own exchange, custodian or wallet, on your instruction. Trnzit never transmits or holds funds.

Multi-signature wallets

Multi-sig, set to your own compliance policy

Use multi-signature wallets configured to your organisation’s own compliance and control policies, with M-of-N approvals and address whitelisting enforced before anything is signed.

  • Address whitelisting: funds can only go to addresses your approvers have agreed

Trade & convert

Trade in your own exchange accounts, from one interface

Convert assets held in an exchange account into any other asset that exchange lists, through its API, without logging in to each one. Orders go to your exchange and are executed there, under your approval policies.

08

How it connects

Secure APIs carry your instructions. Your providers do the rest.

When you approve an action in Trnzit, the instruction is passed through a secure API to the connection you selected: your exchange, custodian or wallet. The provider executes it. Custody never moves.

  1. Step 1

    You

    Members initiate; approvers approve, under your policies.

  2. Step 2

    Trnzit

    Instructions, policies and approvals in one place. No private keys, no custody, no funds.

  3. Step 3

    Secure API

    Your approved instruction is passed to the provider you chose, using access you granted.

  4. Custody stays here

    Your exchange, custodian or wallet

    Your provider executes. Assets never leave its custody, and never pass through Trnzit.

How Trnzit connects, in four steps. One: you, or a member of your organisation, create an instruction such as a payment or trade. Two: Trnzit checks it against your policies (approval thresholds, M-of-N approvals and whitelisted addresses) and collects the approvals required. Trnzit holds no private keys, no custody and no funds. Three: once approved, Trnzit passes the instruction over a secure API to the provider you selected, using access you granted. Four: your exchange, custodian or wallet executes the instruction. Custody stays with your providers throughout. Balances and history flow back to Trnzit for display.
