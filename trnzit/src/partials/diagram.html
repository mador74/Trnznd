How Trnzit connects, in four steps. One: you, or a member of your organisation, create an instruction such as a payment, trade or invoice settlement. Two: Trnzit checks it against your policies (approval thresholds, M-of-N approvals and whitelisted addresses) and collects the approvals required. Trnzit holds no keys, no custody and no funds. Three: once approved, Trnzit passes the instruction over a secure API to the provider you selected, using access you granted. Four: your bank, exchange, custodian or wallet executes the instruction. Custody stays with your providers throughout. Balances and history flow back to Trnzit for display.
