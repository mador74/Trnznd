Changing plan

Upgrade at any time: your new plan and contract start immediately. If you upgrade part way through an annual plan, a new 12-month plan starts at the higher price, and the unused part of your current plan is credited against it. Monthly plans work the same way: the new plan starts immediately at the higher fee, the unused part of your current month is credited against your first new monthly fee, and every month after that is charged on the new plan. Downgrades take effect at your next renewal date.

Set-up takes minutes. As soon as your payment is confirmed, you can start connecting providers and inviting your team.
