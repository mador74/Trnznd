About Trnzit

What is Trnzit?

Trnzit is a treasury management platform from the Trnznd Group. It gives you one login and one view across your crypto exchange accounts, custodians, and software and hardware wallets, with approval policies that control what can happen in them.

Is Trnzit an exchange, wallet provider or custodian?

No. Trnzit is a technology platform only. It isn’t a wallet provider, a custodian, an exchange or an investment platform. Your assets stay with the providers you already use, and those providers carry out every transaction on your instructions.

Does Trnzit give investment advice or manage assets?

No. Trnzit doesn’t advise on, recommend or manage investments. It simplifies and centralises control of the accounts you already hold.

Who is Trnzit for?

Mainly institutional and corporate treasury teams, finance directors and family offices. It’s also useful for experienced individuals with many accounts across exchanges and wallets.

Custody & security

Does Trnzit ever hold my private keys or assets?

No. Trnzit and the Trnznd Group never hold private keys, seed phrases or client assets, and never take custody. You keep control and custody at all times.

If Trnzit doesn’t hold private keys, how do transactions happen?

You (and, for organisations, your approvers) approve an instruction in Trnzit. Trnzit passes it through a secure API to the provider you selected, and that provider executes it. For multi-signature wallets, the signing keys are held by your own members, never by Trnzit.

How does Trnzit stop one person moving funds alone?

Business accounts can set M-of-N approval policies, authority levels and whitelisted addresses, so material actions need more than one person. Initiators can’t approve their own requests, and policy changes need approval too. Individual account holders keep sole control of their own accounts.

Connections

What can I connect?

Crypto exchange accounts, crypto custodians, self-hosted software wallets and hardware wallets. The Free plan lets you add as many of your own software and hardware wallets as you like. Exchange accounts and custodians can be connected on paid plans. See supported providers.

What are payment requests?

A payment request asks someone to pay you, with the amount and the address to pay to. You can create them on every plan. From Business upwards, you can attach a PDF, such as an invoice, to keep your records together.

Which blockchains are supported?

Assets on EVM-compatible chains, Solana and Tron. Assets on other chains aren’t supported at the moment.

Which multi-signature wallets are supported?

Safe (formerly Gnosis Safe) for EVM-compatible assets, and Squads for Solana-based assets. In both, the signing keys are held by your own members, never by Trnzit.

Can I open new accounts through Trnzit?

Trnzit lists exchanges and on- and off-ramp providers you can open accounts with. You open each account directly with the provider, under its own terms and checks, and then connect it to Trnzit. See supported providers.

What does Trnzit store to connect my accounts?

Trnzit stores the API details you provide to connect each account, with the narrowest access each provider allows. It never stores private keys or seed phrases. Pricing & billing

How much does Trnzit cost?

There’s a Free plan with no end date. Business costs $49 a month or $490 a year, and Premium $299 a month or $2,990 a year. Enterprise is priced by negotiation. Annual billing gives you two months free. See Pricing for what each plan includes.

How can I pay?

Prices are in US dollars. You can pay in USD by Visa, Mastercard, Google Pay, Apple Pay or PayPal, or in USDT or USDC. [Confirm accepted USDT/USDC networks]

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan is for one person, or a one-person business: one seat, your own organisation under your business name, and as many of your own software and hardware wallets as you like (MetaMask, Phantom, Ledger and most others) on EVM, Solana and Tron. It includes automatic safety checks on every payment, unlimited payments and receipts, balances refreshed on demand, statement and CSV export, and help-centre and email support (answered within 3 working days). It has no end date.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, 30 days on Business and Premium when you pay by card, Google Pay, Apple Pay or PayPal. Your payment details are authorised when you sign up, but no payment is taken. Cancel within 30 days and you pay nothing; otherwise your first payment is taken on day 31.

If you pay in USDT or USDC, you pay in advance for a monthly or annual plan, and get a full refund if you cancel within 30 days.

Why do I give payment details for a free trial?

So your plan can carry on without interruption if you decide to keep it. Your details are authorised at sign-up, but nothing is taken until day 31, and only if you haven’t cancelled.

How do I cancel?

During a free trial, cancel within 30 days and you pay nothing. If you paid in USDT or USDC, cancel within 30 days for a full refund. The Free plan never needs cancelling. After the trial, cancel with at least 30 days’ notice; the service ends on your renewal date. [Confirm how notice is given]

How long does set-up take?

Minutes. As soon as you sign up, you can start connecting your providers and inviting your team members.

Can I change plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade at any time, and your new plan and contract start immediately. If you upgrade part way through an annual plan, a new 12-month plan starts at the higher price, and the unused part of your current plan is credited against it. Monthly plans work the same way: the new plan starts immediately at the higher fee, the unused part of your current month is credited against your first new monthly fee, and every month after that is charged on the new plan.

Downgrades take effect at your next renewal date.
