Providers you can connect

Connect the accounts you already hold, or open new ones with the providers below to get money in and out, and to buy and sell assets. Every account stays yours, held with the provider you chose.

How it works

  1. Open an account with a provider

    Sign up directly with the provider. Its own terms, identity checks and fees apply.

  2. Connect it to Trnzit

    Add the API details from your provider account. You can revoke them at any time.

  3. Manage it under your policies

    See balances and history in one view, and send instructions under your approval rules.

About these listings

You open and hold each account directly with the provider, under that provider’s own terms, eligibility checks and fees. Trnzit isn’t a party to those accounts, doesn’t hold the funds in them, and doesn’t give advice on which provider to choose.

Trnzit and its affiliates have no commercial or referral arrangements with the providers listed, and receive no fees, commissions or other payment from them. Links are given for information only and aren’t a recommendation. Availability depends on where you live; check with each provider.
