FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is ZEND?

ZEND is a multi-currency digital settlement asset issued by Trnznd, S.A. It is designed to help businesses move, hold and settle value with less reliance on any single national currency.

How is ZEND different from a USD stablecoin?

A dollar stablecoin ties your organisation to one economy’s currency and monetary policy, so any organisation settling outside the dollar takes on currency risk. ZEND is backed by a blended reserve of fiat currencies, so its value is stabilised globally, and its volatility stays low whichever currency you measure it from.

Who issues ZEND?

ZEND is issued by Trnznd, S.A.

Is ZEND fully backed?

Yes. Every ZEND in circulation is backed by a minimum of 100% reserves, held in cash and cash equivalents and segregated from Trnznd’s operational accounts.

What supports the ZEND reserve?

A diversified reserve of major fiat currencies, held as cash and cash equivalents. Reserve attestations and audit reports are published on our transparency page.
