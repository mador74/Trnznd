2. What Trnzit is (and isn’t)

Trnzit is a technology platform only. Trnzit and the Trnznd Group do not provide custodial, money transmission, payment, exchange or investment services, and never hold client assets or private keys. All transactions are executed by your own chosen providers on your instructions.

3. Plans, prices and payment

Normal prices: Basic $29/month or $290/year; Premium $99/month or $990/year; Institution $249/month or $2,490/year. New users, first 12 months: Basic $14.99/month or $149/year; Premium $49.99/month or $499/year; Institution $124.99/month or $1,249/year. A new user is an organisation or individual that has never had a Trnzit account. You’re a returning user if you register with a different email address but connect a bank account, wallet, exchange account or other provider that is, or was, connected to another Trnzit account, or if you previously cancelled a Trnzit plan. If you upgrade during your discount period, you get the discounted price of the higher plan, on the same terms as any other upgrade. The discount still ends 12 months after you first joined. 5a. Changing plan

You may upgrade at any time; the new plan and a new contract begin immediately. If you upgrade part way through an annual plan, a new 12-month plan starts at the higher price, and the unused part of your current plan is credited against it. Monthly plans work the same way: the new plan starts immediately at the higher fee, the unused part of your current month is credited against your first new monthly fee, and every month after that is charged on the new plan. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date.
