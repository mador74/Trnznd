The new “i”: approved

Round dot, approved. The stem matches “r” and “n” (58 units), sits on the same baseline and x-height, and the dot’s top aligns with the ascender of the “t”. Letter spacing to “z” matches the existing z→n gap.

The wordmark vectors were traced from the supplied PNG. A type designer should check them against the master artwork (SVG or AI) before final release.

Usage

Clear space

Keep clear space on all sides equal to the wordmark’s x-height (the height of the “n”). [Assumption: the guide’s Clear Space section has no value]

Minimum size

80px wide for digital, 20mm for print (Guide p.22). Below that, use the Enso symbol alone.

Don’t

Don’t stretch, rotate, recolour, outline, add shadows or effects, or set the wordmark in a font (Guide p.22).
